# Modernization (https://neon1.tech/)

Greenfield, migrate, retrofit, upgrade, or repair: from a full redesign to a targeted fix, one approach or several.

## When it's worth doing

- A server, application, or device is near the end of its support and nobody wants to touch it.
- Running costs keep rising while the system does the same job.
- One person knows how it works, and that knowledge isn't written down.
- A change that should take a day takes weeks, or breaks something else.
- A system that used to work has stopped working properly.

## Five ways to modernize

From the most change to the least. Most projects use one, and some combine several.

- **Greenfield.** Redesign the system and build it new, on a new platform where that fits, with its data and integrations carried over.
- **Migrate.** Move a system to a new home, such as from on-premises servers to the cloud, or from one platform or provider to another, with data reconciled and a rollback plan.
- **Retrofit.** Add what the system lacks without replacing it: sign-in and access control, backups, monitoring, an API, or automated releases.
- **Upgrade.** Improve what's there: supported versions, better performance, lower cost, and cleaner code or configuration.
- **Repair.** Restore what's broken or failing: find the cause, fix it, and test that it stays fixed.
