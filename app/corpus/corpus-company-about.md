# About neon1 (https://neon1.tech/)

A one-person technology practice in New York that leaves people better off with their tech than they were before.

I'm [Pradeep](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pradeep-tathineni/). I've worked with technology and automation for about 15 years, at home and at work. neon1 is how I do that work for local businesses, teams, and families, so they end up better off with their tech than before.

How I work:

- I listen before I build.
- You get your options, even if that's all you need.
- It stays as simple as the problem allows.
- Your accounts, data, and documentation stay yours.
- Costs are explained before work starts.
- I plan for failure and test that the work does what we agreed.

neon1 is the name neon1 LLC, a New York company, does business under. See [the names](names.md) for where it comes from, and [contact](contact.md) to get in touch.
