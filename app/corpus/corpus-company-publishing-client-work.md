# Publishing client work (https://neon1.tech/)

When and how a client's work appears as a case study: permission, anonymity, quotes, and figures.

I publish a client's work only with their permission, in the form they choose. In the [service agreement](service-agreement.md), each client picks yes, decide later, or no for anonymized case studies, and can change it anytime.

- **What appears.** An anonymized study can show the kind of work, year, figures, whether a partner took part, and a broad sector. A name, logo, or quote takes a separate yes.
- **Named, sector only, or anonymous.** A client can be named, or described only by a broad sector such as "an accounting firm," with no detail that could point to them. An anonymous study says only "a small local business" or similar, with no industry.
- **Quotes.** A client's words appear only if they agree, and I reword a quote when they ask me to.
- **Figures.** Results come from the client's own records, or from a short survey where no record exists. The study says which.
- **Private details.** Client data, people's names, and attendee numbers don't appear.
