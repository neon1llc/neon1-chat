# Publishing client work (https://neon1.tech/)

When and how a client's work appears as a case study: permission, anonymity, quotes, and figures.

I publish a client's work only with their permission, and in the form they choose.

- **What appears.** Each client agrees to what a study shows: their name, their sector, a partner firm, the year, the figures, and any quote.
- **Named, sector only, or anonymous.** A client can be named. If they agree, a study can give only a broad sector, such as "an accounting firm", with no name or detail that could point to them. An anonymous study says only "a small local business" or similar, and leaves out the industry too.
- **Quotes.** A client's words appear only if they agree, and I reword a quote when they ask me to.
- **Figures.** Results come from the client's own records, or from a short survey where no record exists. The study says which.
- **Private details.** Client data, people's names, and attendee numbers don't appear.
