# 1NEON (https://neon1.tech/1NEON/)

The neon1 client portal that comes with a client engagement, where each project runs from the first call to handover.

The neon1 client portal at [neon1.tech/1NEON](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/). Each project runs through four layers: Needs, Execute, Observe, and Normalize. Proposals, signed decisions, files, invoices, and results stay together from first call to handover.

- **Available now,** with a client engagement. It isn't sold on its own.
- **Who it helps:** the people at a client organization who follow a project, send files, and approve options, and the administrators who control access.
- **For example,** I issue proposal 0039-r1 with two options. The decision maker compares them and approves 0039-r1-B by signing with a typed name. 1NEON keeps the signature, the time, and a copy of the option's terms as they saw them. The deposit invoice follows in the same project, payable by bank transfer or card.

## Current limits

- Access is by invitation, from neon1 or an administrator of your organization, or through a limited account you make yourself to share your needs before we work together.
- Online payments go through Stripe Checkout, by bank transfer (ACH) or card. Checks, Zelle, and wires are recorded by hand when they arrive.
- An admin can download everything at any time from Settings, as JSON, CSV, and the files as uploaded. A manifest.json lists what's included or left out.
- Texts work only after you agree to them on your profile.
