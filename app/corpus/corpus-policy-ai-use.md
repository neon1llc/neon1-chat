# How I use AI (https://neon1.tech/knowledge/policies/ai-use/)

Where neon1 uses AI tools in its own work, where a person stays responsible, and how your information is kept out of them.

*Last updated November 20, 2026.*

I use AI tools in my own work, mainly for research and review.

## Where I use it

- **Research.** Gathering and condensing sources, standards, and options, which I then review.
- **Understanding a need.** Organizing what I learn in discovery, and checking whether I've missed an angle.
- **Reviewing writing.** Checking documents, including the ones in this Knowledge Center, for gaps and mistakes.
- **Reviewing code.** Looking for bugs, security issues, and missing tests before a change is merged.

## What stays with me

- AI output is one input to my judgment, and the decisions are mine.
- I review everything published or shipped under neon1's name, and I'm accountable for it.
- Facts, figures, and sources are checked against the original before they're used.

## Your information

- Your confidential information, passwords, and payment details don't go into an AI service unless you've approved that service in writing.
- Health information goes into an AI service only if you've approved it and the service is covered by a business associate agreement.

## AI in what I build for you

AI features inside a system I build for you are a separate matter. They're tested and released under the [AI quality and oversight](../standards/ai-quality.md) standard. Neo, the chat on neon1.tech, is an automated assistant and says so.
