## The problem

A local grade school needed a STEM edtech setup teachers would use, on a budget with no room for mistakes. I'd long wanted to do this work in my community.

## The work

I compared options, budgeted the system, and set it up for the classrooms as a volunteer, then invited teachers and parents to free sessions on staying safe online.

## The results

Teachers used it in class, and parents left with specific steps. That showed the help was worth offering more widely.

## After handover

That's how neon1 began, and community work is still part of it. Schools and community groups can ask through [contact](../company/contact.md).
