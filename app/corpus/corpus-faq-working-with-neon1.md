# Working with neon1 (https://neon1.tech/)

Short answers on where, when, how long, cost, and how data is handled.

## Do you come on site?

Yes, within about 30 miles of Queens, NY. Otherwise I work remotely across the US, or on your network through a connection I remove when the job's done. Outside the US, ask.

## When do you work?

Monday to Thursday 6pm to 9pm, Friday 6pm to 8pm, and weekends 8am to 8pm, Eastern. The booking calendar shows what's open.

## Can I start without a call?

Yes. [Start a free 1NEON account](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/?signup=1) and write down what you need, on your own time. When it's ready, ask for a free assessment and book the 30-minute call. I read your project before we talk.

[Free 1NEON accounts](https://neon1.tech/knowledge/guides/free-accounts/) has the details.

## How do you treat our data and accounts?

I use only the access the job needs, keep your information confidential, and remove my access when we're done. We sign a short [service agreement](../engagement/service-agreement.md) once, before any paid work.

Each project is then a [proposal](../examples/proposal.md): one recommendation, or options when there's a real choice. What you approve is what we both agree to. Personal or health data needs the right agreement first, such as a Business Associate Agreement.

## What if something urgent comes up?

I work in planned appointments, with no 24/7 or emergency response. An incident in progress or an outage needs a provider with emergency coverage first.

For anything your business can't go a day without, I take the work only when someone on your side runs it day to day, and the handover is written for that person. A care plan sets my response times in writing, within my working hours.

## How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope, so the proposal gives the schedule. Larger projects run in phases you approve one at a time. I take a few clients at a time, so each gets my full attention.

## How is the cost worked out?

After we talk, I send a short proposal: one recommendation, or options when there's a real choice, each with what it covers, its upkeep, and its cost. You approve it, and the cost changes only if you approve a change.

Every option has the same security baseline: multi-factor sign-in, the least access the job needs, encryption that fits the data, a tested restore, and a way back from every big change. A smaller option does less, not worse. See [how I price my work](../engagement/pricing.md).

## How do invoices work?

Invoices come through [1NEON](../products/1neon.md), in the project they belong to, and are due within 15 days. You can pay by bank transfer or card online, or by check, Zelle, or wire, with no surcharge.

A project usually starts with a deposit, and the rest is billed when you accept the handover. An estimate bills only the hours worked, and never passes its high end without your approval. Anything I buy for you is paid back at cost, with no markup.

## Do you run security training?

I run informational security awareness sessions for groups, from parents and teachers to engineers and executives: phishing, fraud, passwords, networks, and cameras. They're practical, not certified training. For controls, evidence, and audit preparation, see [security readiness](../guides/use-case-security-readiness.md).

## What don't you do?

I don't repair electronics, do licensed electrical or alarm work, issue audit opinions, give legal advice, or handle personal or health data before the right agreements and insurance are in place. I can help you get those agreements.
