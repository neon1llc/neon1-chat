# Working with neon1 (https://neon1.tech/)

Short answers on where, when, how long, how much, and how data is handled.

## Do you come on site?

Yes, within about 30 miles of Queens, NY. I also work remotely, or on your own network through a connection that I set up for the job and remove when it's done.

## When do you work?

Saturdays and Sundays from 8am to 8pm, and weekday evenings from 6pm to 9pm, Eastern time, typically with 10 to 20 hours a week for project work. The booking calendar shows what's open.

## What if something urgent comes up?

I work in planned appointments on weekday evenings and weekends, with no 24/7 or emergency response. For anything your business can't run without for a day, I take the work only when someone on your side runs it day to day. The handover is written for that person: how it works, how to check it, how to restore it, and whom to call for each part. A care plan sets my response times in writing, within my working hours. A security incident in progress or an outage needs a provider with emergency coverage first. Email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech) for anything else.

## Can I start without a call?

Yes. [Start a free 1NEON account](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/?signup=1), write down what your project needs, then ask for an assessment or a call from your account. [Free 1NEON accounts](https://neon1.tech/knowledge/guides/free-accounts/) has the details.

## How long does a project take?

Small jobs take a week or two, medium ones two to four weeks, and bigger projects four to six. I take on up to two clients at a time so each one gets proper attention.

## How is the cost worked out?

After the first call I send a short proposal with options, usually a quick fix, a solid setup, and a fuller build. Each gives a cost estimate, what it covers, the effort, the upkeep, and how it copes with failure. Every option has the same security baseline: multi-factor sign-in, the least access the job needs, encryption that fits the data, a tested restore, and a way back from every big change. A smaller option does less, not worse. You pick. If the scope changes, I tell you before the cost does. See [how I price my work](../company/pricing.md).

## How do you treat our data and accounts?

Before any paid work, we sign a short [service agreement](../policies/service-agreement.md) once, in 1NEON or on paper. It covers your systems and information: only the access the job needs, kept confidential, and removed when we're done. Each project is then a [proposal](../examples/proposal-options.md) with options. Choosing one, signed on paper or in 1NEON, is what we both agree to, including what I need from you. Work with personal or health data needs the right agreement first, such as a Business Associate Agreement.

## How do I pay?

Invoices come through [1NEON](../products/1neon.md), in the project they belong to, and are due within 15 days. Each option is either a fixed price for well-understood work, or an estimate billed for the hours I work, each one listed, that never passes its high end unless you approve a change first. A project usually starts with a deposit (half of a fixed price, or half of an estimate's low end), and the rest is billed once you accept the handover. You can pay online by bank transfer (ACH) or card through Stripe, or by check, Zelle, or wire, with no surcharge. If I buy equipment or software for you, you pay back exactly what the receipt shows, with no markup.

## Do you run security training?

Yes, for groups from parents and teachers to engineers and executives. Sessions cover phishing, fraud, data brokers, passwords, networks, cameras, smart devices, and physical security. For organizations I add privacy policies, data handling, security frameworks, and incident response.

## What don't you do?

I don't repair electronics, though I debug software and hardware installs and get them working together. I don't handle personal or health data until the right agreements, such as a Business Associate Agreement, and appropriate insurance are in place, but I can help you get them. I'm not a lawyer, but I'll work with yours on the technical side.
