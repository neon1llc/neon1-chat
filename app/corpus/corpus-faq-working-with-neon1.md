# Working with neon1 (https://neon1.tech/)

Short answers on where, when, how long, cost, and how data is handled.

## Do you come on site?

Yes, within about 30 miles of Queens, NY. Otherwise I work remotely across the US, or on your network through a connection I remove when the job's done. Outside the US, ask.

## When do you work?

Weekends 8am to 8pm, and weekday evenings 6pm to 9pm, Eastern. The booking calendar shows what's open.

## Can I start without a call?

Yes. [Start a free 1NEON account](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/?signup=1), write down what you need, and ask for an assessment or a call when you're ready.

[Free 1NEON accounts](https://neon1.tech/knowledge/guides/free-accounts/) has the details.

## How do you treat our data and accounts?

I use only the access the job needs, keep your information confidential, and remove my access when we're done. We sign a short [service agreement](../policies/service-agreement.md) once, before any paid work.

Each project is then a [proposal](../examples/proposal-options.md) with options, and the one you choose is what we both agree to. Personal or health data needs the right agreement first, such as a Business Associate Agreement.

## What if something urgent comes up?

I work in planned appointments, with no 24/7 or emergency response. An incident in progress or an outage needs a provider with emergency coverage first.

For anything your business can't go a day without, I take the work only when someone on your side runs it day to day, and the handover is written for that person. A care plan sets my response times in writing, within my working hours.

## How long does a project take?

Small jobs take a week or two, medium ones two to four weeks, and bigger ones four to six. I take up to two clients at a time.

## How is the cost worked out?

After we talk, I send a short proposal with a few options, each with what it covers, its upkeep, and its cost. You pick, and the cost changes only if you approve a change.

Every option has the same security baseline: multi-factor sign-in, the least access the job needs, encryption that fits the data, a tested restore, and a way back from every big change. A smaller option does less, not worse. See [how I price my work](../company/pricing.md).

## How do invoices work?

Invoices come through [1NEON](../products/1neon.md), in the project they belong to, and are due within 15 days. You can pay by bank transfer or card online, or by check, Zelle, or wire, with no surcharge.

A project usually starts with a deposit, and the rest is billed when you accept the handover. An estimate bills only the hours worked, and never passes its high end without your approval. Anything I buy for you is paid back at cost, with no markup.

## Do you run security training?

Yes, for groups from parents and teachers to engineers and executives: phishing, fraud, data brokers, passwords, networks, cameras, and physical security. For organizations I add privacy, data handling, and incident response.

## What don't you do?

I don't repair electronics, give legal advice, or handle personal or health data before the right agreements and insurance are in place. I can help you get those agreements, and I'll work with your lawyer on the technical side.
