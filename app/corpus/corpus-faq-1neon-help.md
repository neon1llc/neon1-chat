# 1NEON help (https://neon1.tech/1NEON/)

Short answers about using the 1NEON client portal.

Signed in? Tap **?** at the top right of any page for help with that page.

## Getting started

### What is 1NEON?
The portal where we run your projects together: what you need, the work, the results, and the handover. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live).

### Can I try it first?
Yes, without signing in. The [guided walkthrough](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live) plays a made-up project on the real portal in about three minutes, and the full story takes about 20 minutes. The [sample project](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/example) is a made-up project to read. The [interactive demo](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/demo/) is a working copy where you pick who to be, and changes stay in your browser until **Start over**. [From sign-up to client](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=free) follows one account from sign-up to client. Short walkthroughs show one thing: [joining from an invitation](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite), [adding a passkey](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey), [adding an authenticator app](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=authenticator), and [texts and text codes](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=texts).

### How do I get in?
When sign-up is open, choose **Create an account** on the sign-in screen. Otherwise, open the link in an invite email. Sign in with a code sent to your email, or with a passkey. There's no password. Watch [creating an account](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=free) or [joining from an invite](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

### I didn't get the sign-in code.
Check spam or promotions, wait a minute, then choose **Send a new code**. A code works for a few minutes, in the browser that asked. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

### Is there a tour?
Yes, at your first sign-in. Replay it from **?** or your profile. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

### What do I do first in a new account?
Outline up to three projects, then ask for an assessment under **Next step** on Home. **Get to know 1NEON** links to a sample project, a walkthrough, the demo, and these FAQs. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=free).

### What is an outline?
What you write about a project before I assess it, in six parts: what it is, how it works today, what done looks like, who and what it touches, a measure, and timing and limits. Every part is optional, and a short answer is enough. **Next step** on Home and the project's **Overview** show what's written, with a tip for each missing part. [What to write](https://neon1.tech/knowledge/guides/free-accounts/#what-to-write) has examples.

### What is Ready for Execute?
The percent on a project's **Overview**, before the work starts. Each outline part is 10, the assessment 10, options 10, and an approved option 20, so 100% needs a decision. Then the outline carries into the work: its measure is tracked in **Observe**, its people and tools shape the handover in **Normalize**, and what done looks like becomes the checks in Verify. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=free).

### How do I ask for an assessment?
Under **Next step** on Home, write the note (required, a sentence or two), choose one to three projects, and choose **Request assessment**, then **Submit request**. It goes to me 30 minutes after your last change, so you can **Edit** or **Withdraw** it until then. An edit after that goes out as an update. I reply by email.

### Do I need a call first?
No. The assessment and the call are separate ways in. **Next step** has **Book a 30-minute call**, or **ask me to email you** (once a week).

### Will I get emails about my outline?
In a new account, yes: while an outline is unfinished and nothing is waiting for me, tips arrive about 3 and 7 days after your last change, then no more. Turn them off under **Outline tips** in your profile's **Notifications**. Clients don't get them unless they turn them on.

### How do I hide or bring back Get to know 1NEON?
Choose **Hide** on the card. To bring it back, open your profile and choose **Show Get to know 1NEON on Home** under Tutorial.

## Projects and the four layers

### What are Needs, Execute, Observe, and Normalize?
The four layers of every project. **Needs** is what you want and the options, **Execute** the work, **Observe** the results against the goals, and **Normalize** the handover, training, and care. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live).

### What should I do first on a project?
Read **Next step** at the top. It says what has to happen now and who it's waiting on. On a new project, **Ready for Execute** says what to write next. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

### What do Not assessed, On track, At risk, and Off track mean?
They're the project's health, as the team sets it. A new project is **Not assessed** until then, and moving stages doesn't change it. Items have their own status: To do, In progress, Blocked, or Done. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What if we rename, reorder, or hide stages?
Projects follow the new names and order. A hidden stage still shows while a project is in it. Decide still asks for an approved option, and Closed still ends a project.

### How do I tell you what I need?
On the **Needs** tab, tap **Add request** and finish a sentence with a few taps. Only the first choice is required. **Define another need** adds more. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=needs).

### Do I have to write a lot?
No. Each section shows an example and one-tap suggestions, and a short answer is enough. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=start).

### How do I know when something changes?
The bell glows. Tap it to see what changed and who changed it. Important changes also email you, and you choose which under **Notifications** in your profile. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=listen).

### How do I comment or change an item?
Tap its title to open its status, owner, due date, details, and comments, which your whole organization sees. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### How do I check something off?
Tick the box beside it. A choice between options counts once one is approved, and a measure at its target. Status updates, decisions, and uploaded files don't count, but a **File needed** does, ticked when you send it. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=handover).

### How do I delete something?
Use the trash button or **Delete** in its panel, and confirm. Approved options and decisions can't be deleted, since they record what was agreed. Files can be deleted by their uploader and admins.

### What does the onion next to a project mean?
Progress. Needs is the center ring and Normalize the outside, each filling as work gets done.

### What does the board show?
Every project as a card in its stage. Drag a card to move it, or use **Move to** on a phone. Move a closed project back the same way, or choose **Reopen**. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What does the timeline show?
Under **Projects**, **Timeline** draws each project from start to target date, colored by layer. The dashed line is today, the diamond the target, and red stripes time past the target. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=care).

### How do I start a project?
Choose **New project** and answer a few questions. It starts with a checklist you can change later. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=start).

### Is there a simpler way for a small job?
Yes. For a fix of a few hours, I start a **Quick fix** with no proposal. I send one quote: an estimate capped at the most hours quoted, or a fixed price when the job is small and well defined. Nothing is paid up front. **Next step** says **Approve the quote**, and you sign once. When it's fixed, you accept that one step with your signature, and I send one invoice. Your organization still signs the service agreement once before its first approval.

### What is the calendar on Home?
Due dates, milestones, project targets, and your organization's events, a month at a time. Choose a day to see them. Editors add leave, closures, meetings, or care plan dates, one-off or repeating. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=calendar).

### What is Insights?
Charts from your organization's data, such as open and overdue work, on-time finishes, and days to a decision. The page explains each number. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=observe).

## Proposals, decisions, and signing

### What is a proposal revision?
My options are issued together as a numbered proposal, such as 0039-r1, each option with its own ID, such as 0039-r1-B. Issuing freezes a copy, so what you approve is what you read. Changed options come in the next revision (0039-r2), and earlier ones stay readable, marked as replaced. Past a revision's price recheck date, approval waits for a refresh. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

### How do I approve an option?
On **Needs**, compare the options and choose **Approve this option**. Add any conditions, type your full name, and tick the box. Your typed name is your signature. The approval records the option's terms as shown (ID, description, price and whether it's fixed or an estimate, hours, rate, timeline, outside costs, and purchases) with your name and the time. To ask for a change, comment on the option or send a marked-up proposal. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Can I download the proposal?
Yes. **Read the proposal**, under the options, puts everything on one page. **Print or save as PDF** keeps a copy, and **Download Word** gives one to mark up. Each page carries the number, such as 0039-r1. Approving in 1NEON counts the same as signing on paper. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### How do I suggest changes to a proposal?
Comment on an option, or mark up the Word download and upload it to **Files**. When 1NEON asks "Is this a revision of Proposal 0039-r1?", choose **Yes**. neon1 answers with the next revision. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Who buys equipment or software for a project?
You do, in your organization's name, unless you agree neon1 does. Expected purchases appear under **Purchases** in Execute once you approve. Mark each bought and **Attach the receipt**. neon1 buys only with the decision maker's signature or initials, and is paid back at cost, with the receipt. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What if we need something new after approving?
Add it as a request. If it changes the agreed scope, it waits for the next revision with its own price, so no work or cost is added without a written change.

### Who can approve?
The project's named decision maker, or, if none, an admin of your organization. Only an admin can name or change the decision maker, under **People** on the overview. Each change is recorded. neon1 can't be named, except in its own organization. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Can I change my mind after approving an option?
Yes, until you accept the handover. The decision maker uses **Choose this instead** on another option, says why, and signs again. Nothing is billed twice: what was invoiced for the earlier option counts toward the new one, hours already worked count toward its hours, and any overpayment comes back as a credit at handover. The earlier decision stays on record, marked replaced, with your reason on the new one. To return to a replaced option, I confirm it in the next revision with today's prices. If no option fits the change, ask me for a new one. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### What does approving an option commit us to?
That your organization agreed to the option as shown: its scope, cost, timeline, and conditions. Both sides can rely on it, alongside your signed agreement with neon1, which governs the paid work.

### How do I accept a handover step?
On **Normalize**, steps are ticked as they're done. Once all are, the decision maker chooses **Accept the handover** and signs with their typed name. One signature accepts every step. **Check it and accept** accepts one step early. Accepted steps can't be edited, and acceptance prompts the final invoice. Watch [the handover](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=handover) and [the final invoice](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=final).

### What do Cost, Quality, and Effort mean?
neon1's 1 to 5 rating of each option, where more dots is always better. **Cost** is the total cost, **Quality** how fully it meets your requirements, and **Effort** your team's effort to adopt and run it. **How this adds up** shows the hours and when you'd pay. Demo and walkthrough figures are illustrative. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

## Files

### How do I send a file?
Use **Upload** next to a file I asked for, or upload in **Files**. Files filed at **Organization level** belong to your whole organization. Choose a project to file under it, in one of its layers or at **Project level** for the project as a whole. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=listen).

### Can I move a file later?
Yes. Choose **Move** on a file and pick Organization level, a project, or a layer. Only where it's listed changes.

### Are files safe?
Every file is virus-checked before anyone can download it and stays private to your organization.

### What can I upload?
A free account holds up to 10 files, 10 MB each and 50 MB in all. A client account takes any file up to 100 MB. What not to upload is in the [terms of use](../policies/terms.md).

## People and access

### What can viewers, editors, and admins do?
Viewers read and comment. Editors also add and change work. Admins also invite people and change access and settings. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=team).

### How do I invite someone?
Admins choose **Invite people** on **People**, and the person gets an email link. If it didn't go out or got lost, choose **Resend**. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=team).

### Can 1NEON text me?
Once texts are on. Add your mobile number on your profile, agree to texts, save, and type the code 1NEON texts you. Texts say only that something new is waiting, with a link, and never name your organization, projects, or files. Reply STOP to turn them off. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=texts).

### Why does it ask me to confirm it's me?
Admin actions (inviting people, changing access or settings, and deleting a project or someone else's comment or file) also need a recent confirmation with a passkey, an authenticator app, or a text-message code. That way a stolen email account isn't enough. Add one under Two-step verification on your profile, and a second is a backup. You won't be asked again for 12 hours in that sign-in. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey).

### Can I use an authenticator app?
Yes. On your profile, choose **Set up** next to Authenticator app, scan the code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, or similar, and type the 6-digit code. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=authenticator).

### What's a passkey?
A sign-in with your face, fingerprint, or device PIN instead of a code. It's phishing-resistant, since it only works on the real 1NEON site. Add one under Two-step verification on your profile. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey).

## Your account

### How do I change my name or the look?
Open your profile from the menu to change your name, title, time zone, menu, or notifications. Most changes save by themselves. The theme and dark mode switches are at the bottom of the menu. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=profile).

### How do I sign out?
Open the menu, tap the sign-out icon beside your name, then tap **Sign out**. Or choose **Sign out** on your profile.

### I lost my phone or my authenticator app.
You can still sign in with an emailed code. The second step is only for admin actions. If you have another method, confirm with it, then remove the lost one and add a new one. If you've lost every method, email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech) from your account's address. Email alone isn't enough, since a stolen mailbox is what the second step guards against, so I confirm who you are by calling a number already on record (never one from the email), plus another admin's written OK if your organization has one. Then I remove the lost methods, record it, and email you. Only admin actions wait meanwhile. There's no self-service reset.

### My email address changed.
Your email is how 1NEON knows you, so you can't change it on your profile. Ask an admin to invite the new address and deactivate the old one, or email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech). Earlier work stays under the old address.

### Who do I ask for access?
An admin of your organization. If you don't know who that is, or neon1's access needs to change, email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech).

## Invoices and paying

### Is a price fixed or an estimate?
Usually an estimate, and the option says which. **How this adds up** shows the work behind it. An estimate is a range of hours at the option's hourly rate, set after discovery. You pay for hours worked, each listed as logged, never past the high end without your approval. Unforeseen work or purchases are agreed before I add them. A small, well-defined job may carry a fixed price instead: the whole price for the work described, changed only by a change you approve. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Where do I see the hours worked?
In **Hours**, under Execute. Each entry has its date, what it went to, and its length in quarter hours. Billed hours carry the invoice's number. On a fixed price, hours are a record, not a bill. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What if the work needs more hours than the estimate?
The high end is the most you pay for the approved work. Hours past it are listed, not billed. If the work itself must change, neon1 says so before the high end and issues a revision with the new price, and nothing is added until you approve. If the work takes less than the deposit covered, neon1 refunds the difference as a credit.

### Where are invoices and payments?
In **Invoices**, under Execute, with what's paid and left on each. Each opens on neon1's letterhead with its number, lines, due date, and how to pay, ready to print or save as a PDF. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=pay).

### How do I pay an invoice?
Choose **Pay**. Stripe's hosted page opens for bank transfer (ACH, preferred) or card, and 1NEON never sees your details. A card shows as paid within a minute. A bank transfer shows as started that day and paid once it clears, usually in a few business days. Check, Zelle, or wire work too, with the invoice number in the memo, and neon1 records them on arrival. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=pay).

### What if my bank payment doesn't go through?
No money moved. The invoice reopens with the failed payment listed, and 1NEON tells you and neon1 that day. Choose **Pay again** or another way to pay. Nothing is added to what you owe.

### Do I get a receipt?
The invoice is your receipt. Once paid it reads **Paid in full**, with each payment's date, amount, and method, and prints or saves as a PDF. Billing contacts are also emailed when a payment arrives.

### Is there a fee for paying by card, or for paying late?
No. The total is what you pay, however you pay, with no card surcharge, late fee, or interest. A late invoice shows as overdue, and neon1 may pause work, after telling you, once one is more than 30 days overdue.

### What if I think an invoice is wrong?
Say so before you pay. Email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech) with the invoice number and line. neon1 fixes it with a credit, or voids it with a reason and issues a new one, on the record.

### When are invoices sent?
As your option's **When you pay** says. A deposit, half a fixed price or half an estimate's low end, comes at approval. The rest comes at handover acceptance: the remainder of a fixed price, or the hours worked less the deposit. Purchases are billed at cost with the receipt, and care plans monthly. Invoices are due in 15 days unless the option says otherwise, and each emails your billing contacts. Watch [the deposit](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=pay) and [the final invoice](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=final).

### Can an invoice be changed?
Not once issued. A mistake is fixed with a credit, or by voiding it with a reason, both on record. An invoice with a payment, or one clearing, can't be voided.

### What is the service agreement?
neon1's terms for all work with your organization: how work is agreed, paying, ownership, confidentiality, and ending it. An admin signs it once in 1NEON, from **Settings**, before the first approval. Read it any time at [/1NEON/agreement](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/agreement). [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

## Support and your data

### Something urgent is wrong. Who do I tell?
Email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech) with what happened and when. neon1 works planned hours with no emergency response, so for something that can't wait, such as bank fraud or an outage, contact the provider responsible first. Report a security problem in the portal itself to [security@neon1.tech](mailto:security@neon1.tech).

### Can we get a copy of our data?
Yes, any time, without asking me. An admin opens **Settings** and chooses **Download everything** under Your data. The zip holds every project's records, decisions and signatures, proposals, invoices, payments, and handover steps as JSON and CSV, plus every comment, the activity history, every uploaded file as uploaded, and projects in Trash. README.txt says what each file holds, and manifest.json lists every file with a SHA-256 checksum. Not included: unissued invoice drafts, files still uploading, and each person's notifications, sign-in methods, and display preferences. If anything is missing, the file ends in -partial.zip instead of -complete.zip, and the README and Settings say what and why.

### What happens to our data when the work ends?
It's kept as the record of our work unless your organization asks me to delete it. Your organization can stop using 1NEON any time and download everything from Settings first. The [privacy policy](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/privacy) has the details, including how long backups and audit records last.

### Can I message you directly?
Yes. Open the round Neo button at the bottom of any page and choose **neon1**. Everyone in your organization sees the conversation, with names, and **private** keeps one message between you and me. You can edit your own messages, and a reply also comes to your email. I pause chat for anyone who spams or abuses it.

### Who can I contact?
Message me in the chat, email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech), or see [Contact](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/contact).
