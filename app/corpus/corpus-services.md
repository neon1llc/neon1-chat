# Services (https://neon1.tech/)

Custom software, workflow automation, cloud and AI engineering, modernization, networks and connected devices, and security, from a focused assessment to a complete implementation.

neon1 is my independent engineering and technology consulting practice. I assess, design, build, integrate, and improve technology systems for businesses, technical teams, and individuals.

## Six services

| Service | What it covers | Common reasons to call |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom software** | Web applications, portals, internal tools, APIs, and services, with tests, a release pipeline, and monitoring | No product fits the work, a spreadsheet or script runs something important, or customers and staff need a portal tied to your data |
| **Workflow automation** | Systems integration, APIs and events, data synchronization and quality, reporting, and AI-assisted document processing | Systems of record disagree, exceptions are handled by hand, reporting needs data no single system holds, or a process has outgrown its tools |
| **Cloud and AI engineering** | Cloud architecture, DevOps (infrastructure as code, CI/CD pipelines, and observability), production readiness, cost, and AI applications with access control, evaluation, and model routing | A launch or migration with a rollback requirement, an architecture or cost review, or an AI feature that must cite sources and respect permissions |
| **Modernization** | Greenfield redesign, migrate to a new home, retrofit missing pieces, upgrade what's there, or repair what's failing, one approach or several | A system near the end of its support, costs that keep rising, knowledge held by one person, or something that stopped working properly |
| **Networks, devices, and connected systems** | Network design and segmentation, multi-site and remote access, IoT, sensors, cameras, and other connected devices, with the hardware and software integrated, monitoring, local storage, and backup | A flat network that should be segmented, a new location, devices to integrate and monitor, or a home network built to a higher standard |
| **Security** | Readiness: risk assessment, identity and access, hardening, backup and recovery, and evidence before a review. Awareness: online, physical, and organizational security sessions for any age or audience | A customer security questionnaire, an upcoming SOC 2, HIPAA, or PCI DSS review, recovery that was never tested, or people who need to spot scams |

## Ways to engage

| Engagement | What you get | Usually priced as |
|---|---|---|
| **Assessment** | A written review of one system, architecture, or risk area: findings, priorities, and costed next steps you can take on with me, with someone else, or yourself | An estimate |
| **Advisory** | A second opinion on a design, vendor, or build-or-buy decision | An estimate |
| **Scoped project** | A defined result, built, tested against agreed acceptance criteria, and handed over | An estimate |
| **Phased project** | Larger work split into phases. A phase is a self-contained piece of up to about six weeks, with its own scope, price, and handover, approved before it starts | Per phase |
| **Ongoing care** | Updates, checks, and set response times within my working hours | A monthly [care plan](../templates/care-plan.md) |

A proposal makes one recommendation, or sets out options when there's a real choice. See [pricing](pricing.md).

The first 30-minute call comes before any of these. Write a project down in a [1NEON account](../guides/free-accounts.md) first, and its assessment is free too: my written read, with a likely approach and the first questions to settle. It's lighter than the paid assessment above.
