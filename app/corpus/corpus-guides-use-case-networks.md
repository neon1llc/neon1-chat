# Networks, devices, and connected systems (https://neon1.tech/)

Designing and integrating networks, devices, and connected environments: when it's worth doing, what I deliver, and how I work with installers and licensed trades.

## When it's worth doing

- A flat network carries point-of-sale, cameras, guests, staff, and smart devices together, and should be segmented.
- A second location, or remote staff, needs consistent design and secure access.
- Cameras, sensors, or building systems need to be integrated with each other or with business systems, and monitored.
- A network grew without a record, and nobody can say what's on it or how to restore it.
- Equipment is about to be bought, and the choice should fit the design and its upkeep.
- A household wants a properly designed network, a home lab, local storage and backup, private camera recording, or secure remote access.

## What I do

- **Design.** Topology, VLAN and IP plans, wireless coverage and capacity, and a firewall policy that keeps device classes apart.
- **Secure access.** Site-to-site and remote-access VPNs, named admin accounts, and no device exposed to the internet that doesn't need to be.
- **Connected devices.** IoT, sensors, and cameras on their own network, with the hardware set up and integrated with your software through vendor APIs or local services, and alerting that people act on.
- **Monitoring and resilience.** Health checks, logs, backup of device configurations, and a tested recovery path.
- **Equipment selection.** Options compared on fit, security updates, lifetime cost, and lock-in.

## What you get

A network diagram, an asset inventory, the configuration with its ownership and admin access recorded, access boundaries, recovery steps, and a maintenance schedule.

## What it needs from you

Access to the current equipment and accounts, the ISP details, and a decision on who administers the network afterward.

## Who does what

I design and configure the network. A qualified installer handles cabling and physical installation, and a licensed installer handles electrical or alarm-system work, arranged per project, with the design and configuration built around their side. For a help desk or on-call device management, a managed service provider pairs well with the design I hand over. On-site work is within about 30 miles of Queens, NY.
