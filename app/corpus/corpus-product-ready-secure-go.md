# Ready, Secure, Go (https://neon1.tech/)

Security readiness for every space, in plain language.

*Public · v0.1.2*

**Security steps for each kind of space.** Plain-language guidance for a phone, a home network, a classroom, a small office, or a company heading into SOC 2: five things worth doing first, then what's next. It's open on [GitHub](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go).

- **What's in it:** ten spaces, from Me to Builders, each with five first steps; a readiness check; practices by level; playbooks for when something goes wrong; teach kits; and printable handouts. It was called neon1 Ready until November 2026.
- **Licenses:** written material under CC BY-SA 4.0, code under MIT.

## Companion guides

Five deeper guides sit alongside it, each on GitHub under the same licenses. They're training material, not legal or audit advice. Have a qualified specialist review anything you rely on for a regulated decision.

- [SOC 2 readiness, step by step](https://github.com/neon1llc/soc-2-readiness): modules, starter controls, and evidence checklists for a company preparing for its first audit.
- [Incident response for small teams](https://github.com/neon1llc/incident-response-for-small-teams): a runbook, playbooks, and tabletop exercises.
- [Compliance basics for small businesses](https://github.com/neon1llc/compliance-basics): short explanations of HIPAA, PCI DSS, privacy, accessibility, and more, as a starting point.
- [A phishing awareness session kit](https://github.com/neon1llc/phishing-awareness-kit): slides, exercises, and a one-page takeaway.
- [A household tech handbook](https://github.com/neon1llc/household-tech-handbook): for the person who looks after a family's devices and accounts.

## Current limits

Its scope is stable. Updates fix accuracy and sources, with no new modules for now, and the same goes for the five companion guides. Settings menus change, so it names where to look, and vendors' help pages have the latest steps.
