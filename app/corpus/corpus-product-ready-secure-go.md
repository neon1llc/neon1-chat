# Ready, Secure, Go (https://neon1.tech/)

Security readiness for every space, in plain language.

*Public · v0.1.1*

**Security steps for each kind of space.** Plain-language guidance for a phone, a home network, a classroom, a small office, or a company heading into SOC 2: five things worth doing first, then what's next. It's open on [GitHub](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go).

- **Who it's for:** households, teachers, small offices, and companies preparing for a security review, who want to know what to do first without the jargon.
- **What's in it:** ten spaces, from Me to Builders, each with five first steps; a readiness check; practices by level; playbooks for when something goes wrong; teach kits; and printable handouts. It was called neon1 Ready until November 2026.
- **An example:** a family opens Home & Family and gets five first steps, such as turning on two-step sign-in for email.

## Companion guides

Five deeper guides sit alongside it, each open on GitHub. They're training material, not legal or audit advice, and a qualified specialist should review anything you rely on for a regulated decision.

- [SOC 2 readiness, step by step](https://github.com/neon1llc/soc-it-2-me): modules, starter controls, and evidence checklists for a company preparing for its first audit.
- [Incident response for small teams](https://github.com/neon1llc/there-are-no-incidents): a runbook, playbooks, and tabletop exercises.
- [Compliance basics for small businesses](https://github.com/neon1llc/you-have-20-seconds-to-comply): short explanations of HIPAA, PCI DSS, privacy, accessibility, and more, as a starting point.
- [A phishing awareness session kit](https://github.com/neon1llc/phish-out-of-water): slides, exercises, and a one-page takeaway.
- [A household tech handbook](https://github.com/neon1llc/home-is-where-the-smart-is): for the person who looks after a family's devices and accounts.

## Current limits

It's a first release (v0.1), so what it covers will grow before v1.0. Settings menus change; it names where to look, and vendors' help pages have the latest steps.
