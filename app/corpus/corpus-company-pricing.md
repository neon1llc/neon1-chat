# How I price my work (https://neon1.tech/)

How a neon1 project is priced: an estimate set after discovery, rough ranges, what the price includes, how changes are agreed, and how paying works.

**The short version**

- Each project is priced in a proposal after we talk, as an estimate that discovery sets, and you can say no at no cost. The ranges here are a rough guide, not a quote. Unforeseen work or purchases are agreed with you before I add them, and if the work takes fewer hours, you pay for fewer.

Every setup is different, so these are rough ranges. The real figure comes in a proposal after we talk.

## How a price is set

Discovery sets the estimate: a range of hours at the proposal's rate. You pay for the hours I work, each listed in [1NEON](../products/1neon.md), and the total never passes the high end without your approval. Unforeseen work or purchases are agreed with you before I add them.

## What projects usually cost

As of November 2026.

| Kind of work | Often around | For example |
|---|---|---|
| A small fix or setup, a few hours | $150 to $750 | A broken sync repaired, or one system's access tightened |
| A focused project, one to four weeks | $1,000 to $3,000 | Two systems kept in sync, or a network segmented with its diagram |
| A larger build or move, several weeks or more | $3,000 or more, in phases you approve one at a time | A portal in your own cloud account, a migration with a tested fallback, or controls before an audit |
| Care after a project | A monthly plan | Updates, checks, and a set response time in a [care plan](../templates/care-plan.md) |

Fictional examples: syncing a retailer's store and point-of-sale on a shared item ID with an exception report is an estimate of $2,400 to $3,000. An independent review of one AWS account before a renewal comes to about $1,500. Segmenting a two-site business's network and linking the sites by VPN, with a diagram and recovery steps, comes to about $2,800. A security questionnaire review comes to about $1,200, then a choice between an estimate of $3,000 to $4,500 to fix the findings together or about $600 for written guidance.

## What's included, and what isn't

A price covers the whole result: working out what's needed, testing it with your real cases, a runbook, a walkthrough for your staff, every account left in your name, and a free fix if something I delivered doesn't work as agreed and you tell me within 30 days of handover.

Separately, subscriptions and hardware are yours, billed by the vendor where possible, and the proposal lists each with an estimate. Anything I buy for you is paid back at exactly what the receipt shows, with no markup, and only after you've agreed to it.

## When the price can change

Only with your approval. If the work turns out bigger, or something unforeseen or a purchase comes up, I tell you before it costs more, and a revised proposal shows the new estimate. If the work takes fewer hours, you pay for fewer, and if the deposit came to more than the finished work, I refund the difference. Until you accept the handover, you can pick another option, say why, and sign again. What you've paid counts toward it, and nothing is billed twice.

## Paying

A project usually starts with a deposit of half the estimate's low end, and the rest is billed when you accept the handover. Invoices come through 1NEON, due within 15 days, by bank transfer, card, check, Zelle, or wire, with no surcharge. The [service agreement](service-agreement.md) sets this out.

## Room to adjust

These ranges aren't a floor. I sometimes price below them for a neighborhood business, a nonprofit, or work that helps people who'd otherwise go without, and the proposal says so. Every proposal shows the hours behind each price.

## How this compares

Published 2026 figures: general IT consulting firms quote about $150 to $300 an hour, a simple automation from a freelancer runs about $500 to $1,500, and managed IT for a small New York office runs about $85 to $200 per person each month.
