# How I price my work (https://neon1.tech/)

How a neon1 project is priced: a first call, a written estimate for each piece of work, changes agreed before they are added, approval before work starts, and how paying works.

**The short version**

- Every piece of work is priced in writing before it starts: a first call, a written estimate with the hours behind it, changes agreed before they are added, and your approval to begin. Larger efforts are priced phase by phase. If the work takes fewer hours, you pay for fewer.

Every setup is different, so I price each piece of work on its own, in writing, before it starts. Nothing here is a quote. The figure comes in a proposal, after we've talked.

## How a price is set

1. **A first call.** We talk about what you need and what done looks like. It's free, and it doesn't commit you to anything.
2. **A written estimate.** I send a proposal with the work, the hours behind each price, and the schedule. The price is an estimate: a range of hours at the proposal's rate, set after discovery. You pay for the hours I work, each listed in [1NEON](../products/1neon.md), and the total never passes the high end without your approval.
3. **Phases for larger work.** A bigger build or move is split into phases. Each phase has its own scope, estimate, and handover, and you approve it before it starts.
4. **Changes agreed first.** Nothing is added to the estimate until you've agreed to it. [More below](#when-the-price-can-change).
5. **Your approval to begin.** Work starts after you approve an option and sign.

## What moves a price

How many systems are involved, how much of your real data and edge cases need testing, how carefully a cutover has to be staged, whether it runs in your own cloud account, and how much your staff needs to be walked through. A proposal names which of these drive the estimate.

## What's included, and what isn't

A price covers the whole result: working out what's needed, testing it with your real cases, a runbook, a walkthrough for your staff, every account left in your name, and a free fix if something I delivered doesn't work as agreed and you tell me within 30 days of handover.

Separately, subscriptions and hardware are yours, billed by the vendor where possible, and the proposal lists each with an estimate. Anything I buy for you is paid back at exactly what the receipt shows, with no markup, and only after you've agreed to it.

After a project, an optional [care plan](../templates/care-plan.md) covers updates, checks, and a set response time.

## When the price can change

Only with your approval. If the work turns out bigger, or something unforeseen or a purchase comes up, I tell you before it costs more, and a revised proposal shows the new estimate. If the work takes fewer hours, you pay for fewer, and if the deposit came to more than the finished work, I refund the difference. Until you accept the handover, you can pick another option, say why, and sign again. What you've paid counts toward it, and nothing is billed twice.

## Paying

A project usually starts with a deposit of half the estimate's low end, and the rest is billed when you accept the handover. Invoices come through 1NEON, due within 15 days, by bank transfer, card, check, Zelle, or wire, with no surcharge. The [service agreement](service-agreement.md) sets this out.

## Room to adjust

I sometimes price lower for a neighborhood business, a nonprofit, or work that helps people who'd otherwise go without, and the proposal says so. Every proposal shows the hours behind each price.
